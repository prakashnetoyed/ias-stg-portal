Privacy Policy

The Indian Administrative Service Central Association (IASCA) is committed to safeguarding the privacy of its members. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the personal information of IAS officers who interact with the Association.

Information We Collect

Information is collected from two primary sources:

  1. From the Department of Personnel & Training (DoPT) – Basic officer details such as name, cadre, batch, designation, and service records as available.
  2. Direct Submission by Members – Information manually provided by officers while filling membership forms, updating profiles, or contributing to the Welfare Fund.

The information may include:

  • Name, service batch, cadre, and designation.
  • Contact information (email address, phone number, address).
  • Payment details (processed securely via third-party gateways; IASCA does not store card/banking credentials).
  • Any additional information voluntarily shared by the officer.

How We Use the Information

The collected information is used solely for:

  • Processing and maintaining membership records.
  • Acknowledging and tracking Welfare Fund contributions.
  • Sending official notices, circulars, or updates related to IASCA activities.
  • Maintaining accurate internal databases for Association purposes.
  • Complying with any statutory or regulatory requirements.

Information Sharing & Disclosure

  • IASCA does not sell, rent, or trade member information.
  • Data may be shared only with trusted service providers (such as secure payment gateways or IT support) to enable necessary services.
  • Information may be disclosed if required by law, government orders, or regulatory authorities.

Data Security

  • IASCA implements strict administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information.
  • Access to member data is restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • While we take reasonable steps to secure information, members are advised to take precautions when accessing the IASCA portal.

Cookies & Website Tracking

The website may use cookies or similar tools for smooth navigation and improved functionality.

Members can manage or disable cookies via browser settings, though some features may be impacted.

Your Rights

Members have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal information maintained by IASCA.
  • Request corrections or updates to their details.
  • Opt out of non-essential communications by notifying the Association.

Updates to the Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Updates will be posted on the IASCA website with the date of the latest revision.
